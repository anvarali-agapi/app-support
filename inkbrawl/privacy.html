Privacy Policy

Inkbrawl: Pen Fight Battles · Effective November 4, 2026

Summary

Inkbrawl does not collect, store or share any personal data. It needs no account, shows no ads, sells nothing and makes no network requests.

What the app stores on your device

To remember your game, the app saves your progress (stars, unlocked pens, ink colour, match records and your last squad) and your settings (music, sound effects, haptics and the aim ghost) locally on your device. This data is never uploaded, is not linked to you, and is deleted when you delete the app or reset progress in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics and no crash reporting services
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No in-app purchases and no payment details
  • No network requests of any kind
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling of data
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
