Inkbrawl

Pen Fight Battles. Flick, spin, and knock every pen off the desk. A pen-flicking action-strategy game for iPhone and iPad.

Need help or found a bug?

Open a support request and describe what happened. Please include your device model, iOS version and the desk number (for example 2-4) if your question is about a specific level. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

On your turn, drag back from one of your pens like a slingshot and let go. The further you pull, the harder the flick. The ghost path shows where the pen goes up to its first impact.

Where you grab matters. Hold the middle for a straight shove; hold an end and the pen spins, sweeping everything it touches. There is no rail around the desk: any pen whose centre crosses the edge falls and is out, yours included. If your last pen falls on your own flick, you lose.

Pens and abilities

Every pen has one ability per battle. Tap it in the tray to arm it, then flick. The Clicker flicks twice (Double Tap). The Glider leaves a slippery ink trail. The Brute caps down and cannot be moved until your next turn. The Noble whirls like a propeller (Spin Shot). The Ricochet hits like a pen twice its weight (Sharpen). The Wide's Shove pushes every nearby pen away on a second tap instead of a flick.

Reading the desk

Rulers are walls. Erasers and books soak up hits. Tape rolls bounce. Ink puddles are slippery, so pens keep sliding; glue blobs stop them dead. Each briefing screen lists what is on the desk before you choose your squad.

Stars, pens and ink colours

Finish a desk within its par number of flicks for three stars, within par plus two for two stars. New pens unlock as you clear desks 1-3, 1-6, 2-4, 3-2 and 4-3. Ink colours in the Pen Case unlock with your total stars. Later rooms open when you clear the room before.

Versus

Versus mode plays on any desk you have reached. Fight the AI at Rookie, Sharp or Ace, or choose 2 players and pass the device between flicks. Player 1 flicks from the bottom and Player 2 from the top.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Stars, unlocked pens, ink colours, match records and settings are saved automatically on your device. Progress is not synced between devices, and deleting the app removes it. Settings has a Reset progress option.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Inkbrawl needs no account or sign-in and plays fully offline. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.
