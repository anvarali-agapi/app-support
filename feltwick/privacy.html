Privacy Policy

Feltwick · Effective November 7, 2026

Summary

Feltwick does not collect, store or share any personal data. It needs no account, shows no ads, sells nothing and makes no network requests.

What the app stores on your device

To remember your game, the app saves your progress (the quilt in progress, Thimbles, unlocked cards, heirlooms and kits, finished patterns and the Journal statistics) and your settings (music, sound effects, haptics and hints) locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. This data is never uploaded, is not linked to you, and is deleted when you delete the app or choose Reset all progress in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics and no crash reporting services
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No in-app purchases and no payment details
  • No network requests of any kind
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling of data
  • No device permissions: no access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
