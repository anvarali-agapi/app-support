Feltwick

Cozy Deck Builder. Sew a village onto Grandma's quilt one card at a time, meet her commissions, and unlock more of the supply closet. A cozy roguelite deck-building village game for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Need help or found a bug?

Open a support request and describe what happened. Please include your device model and OS version, and the chapter and evening if your question is about a specific moment in a run. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Each evening you draw a hand of cards and have three Thread to spend. Tap a card, then tap a glowing patch to sew it onto the quilt. Buildings make Cozy (your score) and Buttons (your money) at the end of every evening, and where you put them matters: Cottages love a Garden or Pompom Tree next door, Lanterns light the homes around them, Libraries want quiet, and Mills are noisy neighbours. Tap any building to see exactly what it will make tonight and why.

A quilt is three chapters of five evenings. At the end of each chapter Grandma checks your total Cozy against her Commission, shown on the measuring tape at the top of the screen.

What happens between chapters?

A Rest Stop: choose one card to add to your deck, buy cards or unpick weak ones in the Closet Shop, pick an heirloom (a relic that lasts the whole run), and sometimes meet a little story with a choice to make. Then the weather for the next chapter is revealed. Weather changes the rules for one chapter; Moth Season, for example, chews a new Tangle into the quilt every evening.

I missed a commission. Did I lose everything?

The quilt is put away and that run ends, but you keep the Thimbles it earned: one per ten Cozy, a few per finished chapter, and ten more for a finished quilt. Spend Thimbles in the Closet on new cards, heirlooms and starter kits, which stay unlocked forever.

What are Tangles and fabrics?

Tangles are knots of yarn you cannot build on until Scissors or a Lint Roller clear them. The quilt is patched from Cotton, Velvet, Denim, Lace and Burlap, and some buildings love a particular fabric. Tap an empty patch to read about it, or open the Pattern Book from the home screen for the full reference.

What is Overtime?

When you meet the third commission the quilt is finished. You can frame it and bank your Thimbles, or keep sewing into Overtime chapters that ask for more and more Cozy. The run ends the first time you fall short, and either way the quilt counts as finished.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The quilt on the table is saved after every stitch, and Thimbles, unlocks and the Journal are saved automatically on your device. Progress is not synced between devices, and deleting the app removes it. Settings has a Reset all progress option.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Feltwick needs no account or sign-in and plays fully offline. It has no ads and no in-app purchases. Music, sound effects, haptics and the first-quilt hints can be switched off in Settings or from the in-run menu.
