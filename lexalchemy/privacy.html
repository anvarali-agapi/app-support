Lexalchemy

Privacy policy · effective 9 November 2026

Summary

Lexalchemy does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small save on your device so your workshop survives between sessions. It contains only game data:

  • which vials you have solved, the fewest moves you used and the grade earned
  • your essence balance and the words you have formed (shown in the Grimoire)
  • Daily Elixir streak, the date of the last elixir brewed and that chain of words, and the count of elixirs brewed
  • Athanor best score, rounds reached and number of runs
  • counters: vials solved, moves made, reagents used, and whether the first lesson is done
  • your settings: ambience, sound effects and haptics

This data never leaves your device. It is not linked to you, and deleting the app deletes it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • It does not connect to the internet. The daily puzzle is computed on your device from the calendar date.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
  • It does not read your clipboard or any file outside its own save.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Lexalchemy is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
