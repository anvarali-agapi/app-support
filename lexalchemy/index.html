Lexalchemy

Word ladder puzzle · support

What it is

Lexalchemy is a word ladder puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Android, set in an alchemist's workshop. Each vial gives you a starting word and a target word: change one letter at a time, passing only through real words, and reach the target before your moves run out. COLD → CORD → CARD → WARD → WARM. It is free, works offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

Contact support

Found a bug, a word you think should be accepted, or an idea? Open an issue and we will answer there. Please include your device model, your iOS or Android version, and the chapter and vial number (shown at the top of the screen).

How to play

  • Tap a rune of the current word, then tap a letter on the keyboard to transmute it. The new word must be a real word that has not already appeared in the chain.
  • Or tap a letter first: if it makes exactly one word, it is placed for you; if it fits in several places, the possible runes glow violet and you choose.
  • Undo is free and gives the move back. Reset returns to the starting word.
  • The small cauldron beside the moves gauge glows emerald while a solution still exists within your remaining moves, and turns to grey smoke when the mixture is inert.
  • A rune under red wax is sealed and cannot be changed in that vial. A forbidden word cannot be formed, even when it lies on the shortest road.
  • Finish in par or fewer moves for gold, one over for silver, anything else within the limit for lead.
  • Essence is earned from every solved vial, daily elixir and Athanor run. Spend it on Mercury (shows which rune to change), Sulphur (makes the next word) or Salt (one extra move).

Common questions

A real word was rejected. Words are judged against a list of common English words plus their plurals; proper names, abbreviations and very obscure words are not reagents. If a common word is missing, tell us through the link above and we will add it.

The cauldron turned to smoke. No chain of real words reaches the target in the moves you have left. Undo one or more steps, or reset the vial.

How do I open the next chapter? Transmute ten vials in the current chapter. The first three vials of a chapter are open; after that each vial opens when the one before it is solved.

I missed a day. Is my streak gone? Yes, the Daily Elixir streak counts consecutive days. Your total of brewed elixirs is kept.

What is the Athanor? An endless furnace: vessels arrive one after another with only one move to spare over par. An inert vessel costs one of three flasks; when the last breaks the run ends and the score is recorded.

Can I turn off the sound? Yes. Ambience, sound effects and haptics each have a switch in Settings.

Does it work on iPad? Yes, the layout adapts to iPhone and iPad screens in portrait.

Saving and progress

Your workshop (solved vials and their grades, essence, every word you have formed, daily streak, Athanor best score and settings) is saved on your device after every solved vial and when the app goes to the background. A vial in progress is not saved; leaving it returns you to the shelf. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes it on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Lexalchemy works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
