Voltrace

What it is

Voltrace is a cyberpunk dash-and-slash survival game for iPhone, iPad and Android. You never walk: every swipe is a dash, every dash leaves a burning light trace, and the swarm never stops coming. Survive ten waves, beat the Warden, reach dawn, unlock the next district. It is free, offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

How to play

  • Swipe anywhere to dash. Anything on your path is slashed, and you cannot be hit while dashing.
  • Your dash leaves a light trace that burns enemies crossing it. Draw walls, then let them come.
  • Tap to pulse: a shockwave that knocks enemies back and clears bullets.
  • Charge is both your health and your stamina. It drains every second, each dash sips it, each hit gulps it. Kills drop volts that refill it, so keep killing and keep moving.
  • Every level you pick one of three augments for this run. Between runs, spend memory shards in the Neural Core for permanent upgrades, or unlock a new runner.
  • The Sentinel guards wave 5, the Warden guards wave 10. Beat the Warden to reach dawn and unlock the next district.

Common questions

My charge empties too fast. Standing still is what kills you. Dash through groups to slash them, and collect the volt orbs they drop. The Battery and Slow Drain nodes in the Neural Core give you more room.

The screen went dark. That is the Chrome Bazaar blackout hazard: only your trace and pulses light the arena. It passes after a few seconds.

How do I unlock the next district? Reach dawn (kill the Warden on wave 10) in the district before it.

Can I turn off the sound or the screen shake? Yes. Music, sound effects, haptics and screen shake each have a switch in Settings and in the pause menu.

Does it work on iPad? Yes, the layout adapts to iPhone and iPad screens in portrait.

Saving and progress

Progress (memory shards, Neural Core upgrades, unlocked runners and district records) is saved on your device automatically after every run. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset progress wipes it on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Voltrace works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
