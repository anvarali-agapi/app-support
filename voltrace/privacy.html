Voltrace

Privacy policy · effective 7 November 2026

Summary

Voltrace does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file on your device so your progress survives between sessions. It contains only game data:

  • memory shards, Neural Core upgrade ranks, unlocked and selected runners
  • per-district records (best wave, dawns reached, runs played)
  • whether the tutorial has been completed
  • your settings: music, sound effects, haptics and screen shake

This data never leaves your device. It is not linked to you, and deleting the app deletes it. Settings › Reset progress erases it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
  • It does not read your clipboard or any file outside its own save.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Voltrace collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains no chat, no links to social networks and no purchases.

Changes

If a future version ever changes how the game handles data, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue or see the support page.
