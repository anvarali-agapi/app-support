Runewall

Match 3 Defense Saga. Match runes to feed your wards, hold the road to the Hall, and stand through the long night before Ragnarök. A Norse puzzle tower defence game for iPhone and iPad.

How do I play?

Swap two neighbouring runes on the board to line up three or more of the same kind. Each rune you clear adds fervor to the matching ward's pool on the wall, and wards only fire while their pool has fervor. Fehu runes pay gold instead. Tap an empty socket beside the road to build a ward there (40 gold), and tap a built ward to upgrade or sell it.

Matching four runes in a line makes a Bindrune, an L or T shape makes a Thunderstone, and five in a line makes Odin's Eye. Clearing a special rune also looses a Wrath of its element across the whole battlefield.

My ward is not firing

Its pool is empty. Match its rune to refill it. The pool glyph on the wall turns red while a ward is starving, and a horn sounds when every ward of an element has run dry.

What are the frozen runes?

Hel's curse. Every foe that reaches the Hall frosts one rune on the board, and frosted runes cannot be swapped or matched. Clear a rune next to a frosted one to thaw it. Some battles start with frosted runes already on the board.

Warband, spells and Valor

Before each battle you choose a warband of three to five wards. Only the runes of those wards appear on the board, so a smaller warband cascades more often but has fewer answers. Cascades fill the Valor meter, which pays for spells such as the healing of the Hall or a field-wide frost.

Sagas, bosses and Ragnarök

There are thirty battles across five sagas, each ending in a boss. Battles unlock in order, and new wards and spells join your Hall as you win. Ragnarök is an endless siege that unlocks after you defeat Fenrir in battle twelve; your best wave is recorded in Settings.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Stars, unlocks, Ragnarök records, your Hall banner and settings are saved automatically on your device. Progress is not synced between devices, and deleting the app removes it. Settings has a Reset progress option.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Runewall needs no account or sign-in and plays fully offline. It has no ads and no in-app purchases. Music, sound effects and haptics can be switched off in Settings or from the pause menu.
