Privacy Policy

Chalkboard FC · Effective 10 October 2026

The short version

Chalkboard FC does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the best number of touches for each level you have finished.
  • Your settings: sound, vibration, and which tutorials you have seen.
  • Power-up charges and when the next one refills.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and Settings → Reset progress clears it at any time.

Network and permissions

The game works fully offline. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the game is suitable for players of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
