Chalkboard FC

One-touch football passing puzzles, drawn on a coach's chalkboard.

How do I play?

Touch anywhere, drag back and let go to kick. When the ball rolls into a teammate's ring, touch the screen: time slows down while you aim their one-touch pass. Score in par touches or fewer for three stars. Tap the ? on the home screen for the full guide.

What are power-ups?

Coach's Eye shows the full path of a kick, the Whistle freezes defenders and the keeper, and Rewind undoes your last touch. You hold up to three of each, and one comes back every 15 minutes, even while the game is closed. Power-ups are free; there are no purchases.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. There is no account. Deleting the app removes your progress. You can reset it any time in Settings.

A level seems impossible

Every level is checked to be solvable in par touches. Try the Whistle on levels with moving defenders, or Coach's Eye to see bank shots.
