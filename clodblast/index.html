CLODBLAST

What it is

Clodblast is a claymation puzzle platformer with a boomer shooter's weapons, for iPhone, iPad and Android, played in landscape. Push and shove clay crates like Sokoban, nail precise jumps and blast jumps, and splatter clay monsters across 20 hand-built levels in four worlds, ending with a boss called the Smudge. It is free, works offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

Contact support

Found a bug, stuck on a puzzle, or have an idea? Open an issue and we will answer there. Please include your device model, your iOS or Android version, and the level number (for example 2-3).

How to play

  • Move: hold the left or right half of the pad at the bottom left.
  • Jump: the teal button. Tap for a hop, hold for a full jump.
  • Fire: the red button. Tap or hold to shoot; it aims at the nearest enemy in front of you. Drag it like a stick to aim anywhere, straight down included.
  • Weapons: tap the round slots above the buttons to switch: Thumbgun, Squelcher, Pebblegun and Clodzooka.
  • Crates: walk into a crate to push it one tile. You can push only one at a time and crates fall. Drop them into spike pits, onto plates or onto enemies.
  • Shoving: two Squelcher pellets on a crate, or a Clodzooka or keg blast beside it, shove it one tile away from you.
  • Plates and gates: a gate stays open only while something heavy sits on its plate.
  • Kegs and kiln bricks: shoot a pop keg to blow it up (stand back). Kiln bricks only break in a blast.
  • Blast jump: with the Clodzooka, drag FIRE straight down and shoot. Your own clods never hurt you.
  • Flags: touch a checkpoint flag to save your spot. The flag button at the top right puts the level back as it was at the flag.
  • Keyboard: arrows or A/D move, Space jumps, J or F fires, hold S to aim down, 1 to 4 pick weapons, Esc pauses.

Common questions

A crate is stuck and I cannot finish the puzzle. Press the flag button at the top right, or Back to flag in the pause menu. Everything returns to how it was when you touched the last flag.

I cannot shove a crate. Two Squelcher pellets from the same shot must hit it, the space beyond it must be free, and the crate must not be falling. Shots aimed steeply up or down do not shove.

The wall will not break. Cracked clay breaks after three hits from any weapon. Red kiln bricks only break in a Clodzooka or keg blast.

How do I get all three thumbprints? Clear the level, squish every enemy, and find every golden secret. Secrets hide on high ledges and behind walls with a faint crack.

How do I unlock clay colours? Thumbprints unlock them in the Workshop.

The Smudge keeps hitting me. Keep moving, jump over its ground waves when it slams down, and shoot the Flappers it calls before they reach you. Tapping FIRE aims at it for you.

Can I change the controls? Settings has auto-aim, three button sizes, haptics and screen shake.

Does it work on iPad? Yes, in landscape, with the layout scaled for the larger screen.

Saving and progress

Cleared levels, thumbprints, best times, clay colours, field notes and settings are saved on your device when you finish a level and when you change a setting. A level in progress is not saved: closing the app mid-level restarts that level next time. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes it on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Clodblast works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
