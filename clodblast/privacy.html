CLODBLAST

Privacy policy · effective 7 November 2026

Summary

Clodblast does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small save on your device so your progress survives between sessions. It contains only game data:

  • for each level you have played: thumbprints earned, best time, whether you beat par, and your best enemy and secret counts
  • the clay colour you chose, and which weapons and enemies you have met (for the field notes)
  • counters: enemies squished, crate squashes and splats, and whether the control hints have been shown
  • your settings: music, sound effects, haptics, screen shake, auto-aim and button size

This data never leaves your device. It is not linked to you, and deleting the app deletes it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
  • It does not read your clipboard. It only writes to it when you tap Copy link in Settings.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Clodblast is rated for ages 9 and up for mild cartoon violence: clay monsters burst into clay chunks, with no blood. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
