Privacy Policy

Rivet Runaway · Effective October 9, 2026

Summary

Rivet Runaway does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline.

What the app stores

To remember your game, the app saves your level progress (stars, unlocked levels and which gameplay tips you have already seen) and your settings (music, sound effects and haptics) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not linked to you, and is deleted when you delete the app or reset progress in Settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No network connections, in-app purchases or third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because no personal information is collected, none is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
