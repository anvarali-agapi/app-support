Rivet Runaway

Unscrew. Unblock. Escape. A puzzle game where you free plates and sort screws so Rivet the little robot can reach the exit.

How do I play?

Tap a screw to unscrew it. When a plate loses all its screws, it falls away. Clear the plates covering Rivet's glowing path to reach the exit. Each screw flies into the box of its colour; three screws fill a box. If there is no matching box, the screw waits in the tray, and if the tray fills up, the level is lost. Escape in par moves to earn three stars. The in-game "How to play" screen explains it step by step.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your stars and unlocked levels are saved automatically on your device. Progress is not synced between devices, and deleting the app removes it. You can reset it any time in Settings.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Rivet Runaway works fully offline, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

Reporting a problem

Email kristidenna@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version and the level number, and describe what happened. We usually reply within a few days.
