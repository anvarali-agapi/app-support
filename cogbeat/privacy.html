Cogbeat

Privacy policy · effective 7 November 2026

Summary

Cogbeat does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small save on your device so your progress survives between sessions. It contains only game data:

  • your Cogs balance, Forge ranks, weapons owned and the weapon equipped
  • which districts are unlocked, the district you last played, and per-district records (best wave, best Cadence, cleared, runs)
  • counters: machines scrapped, shifts worked, shifts cleared, best Cadence overall, and whether the control hints have been shown
  • your settings: music, sound effects, haptics, VENT button and beat flash

This data never leaves your device. It is not linked to you, and deleting the app deletes it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
  • It does not read your clipboard or any file outside its own save.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Cogbeat is rated for ages 9 and up for mild cartoon violence against clockwork machines. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
