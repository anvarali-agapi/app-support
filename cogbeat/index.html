What it is

Cogbeat is a twin-stick shooter that runs on a metronome, for iPhone, iPad and Android. Your weapon fires on its own rhythm pattern, the downbeat doubles your damage, and a dash timed to the beat is a free Cadence Step. Work six factory districts, each with its own tempo, hazard and Foreman, level up mid-shift, and spend Cogs in the Forge and Armory. It is free, offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

How to play

  • Left half of the screen: a stick appears under your thumb. Push to walk.
  • Right half: another stick aims. While you aim, the weapon fires by itself on its rhythm pattern (the Riveter on the eighths, the Scattergun on beats one and three, and so on).
  • Hammerfall: the first beat of every bar. Shots fired on it hit twice as hard and the floor flashes.
  • Dash: flick the movement stick, or tap VENT. You are untouchable while dashing and shatter bullets you pass through. Dash on a beat (the ring at the top is your metronome) and it is a Cadence Step: free, it slashes enemies on your path and adds Cadence. Off the beat it costs one steam charge; steam refills one charge every two beats.
  • Cadence is your combo: kills +1, Hammerfall kills +3, Cadence Steps +2. Each point adds 4% damage, up to triple at 50 (Overdrive: your shots pierce). Getting hit halves it.
  • Waves: hatches glow for a bar before they open. Wave eight is the Foreman; it stomps on every Hammerfall, so dash through the ring. Scrap it to open the next district.
  • Levels and Mods: kills give XP; each level offers three Mods for the rest of the shift.
  • Cogs: dropped by machines and paid per wave and Foreman. Spend them in the Forge (permanent ranks) and the Armory (weapons).

Common questions

My shots are not firing. The weapon only fires while the right stick is held, and only on its pattern slots. The Harpoon Lance fires once a bar, on the Hammerfall.

The dash says NO STEAM. An off-beat dash costs a charge. Dash on a beat (Perfect or In Time) and it is free even with no steam.

The Foreman's ring keeps hitting me. Dash through it: a dash is invulnerable. The Foreman lifts on beat four and stomps on beat one, so flick on the one.

I cannot see the enemies in the Undercroft. That is the district's hazard: the lamps are out and every beat lights the room for a moment. Count the beats and shoot on the flash.

Do I lose my Cogs when I die? No. Every shift pays, cleared or not.

Can I turn off the sound, the flash, or the VENT button? Yes. Music, sound effects, haptics, the VENT button and the beat flash each have a switch in Settings.

Does it work on iPad? Yes, the layout adapts to iPhone and iPad screens in portrait.

Saving and progress

Your Cogs, Forge ranks, weapons, unlocked districts, records and settings are saved on your device at the end of every shift and after every purchase. A shift in progress is not saved: closing the app ends it (the pause menu lets you clock out deliberately). There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes it on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Cogbeat works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
