Hushreef

Rhythm Hide & Dive. Listen for the predator's motif, hide on the beat, dive deeper. An underwater music arcade game for iPhone and iPad.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to swim. Glowing notes drift past; collect them to build up the music and your combo. Tap a hideout within reach to tuck into it, and tap anywhere to leave. Tap open water to dash, and dash on the beat for a bonus.

Each predator plays its motif four beats before it arrives, and a banner shows what is coming. Sharks sweep the whole screen, so hide. Eels slither down one column and pass straight through kelp, so hide in a shell, anemone or cave, or move aside. Anglers sweep a light cone, so hide or stay out of the beam.

Hide early and you are safe. Hide in the last beat before the predator arrives and you score a Perfect Hush. Getting caught costs a bubble and halves your combo; lose all three bubbles and the song ends.

Hideouts

Anemones close for a while after you leave them. Shells may already have a crab inside, which your glow reveals as you get close. Kelp does not protect you from eels. Sea cucumbers look like hideouts but are not. Deeper down, some hideouts only show themselves inside your glow.

Chapters, lost chords and Deep Drift

There are thirty songs across five chapters. Songs unlock in order as you finish the one before. Each song hides a lost chord in a pocket of the reef wall for players who leave the main lane. Deep Drift is an endless descent that unlocks later and speeds up stage by stage, with an extra bubble each stage.

The beat feels late

Every device has a little audio delay. Open Settings, choose Calibrate and tap along with the ticks. The game then shifts its beat timing to match your device and headphones.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Stars, scores, lost chords, glows and settings are saved automatically on your device. Progress is not synced between devices, and deleting the app removes it. Settings has a Reset progress option; sound settings stay.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Hushreef needs no account or sign-in and plays fully offline. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.
