What it is

Kaanch is a cozy puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Android built on the old Indian courtyard game of picking up broken glass bangle pieces. Every round a heap of curved glass arcs tumbles onto a sunlit terracotta floor. Press and hold a piece that nothing rests on: a brass ring fills around your finger and, if your hand stays still, the piece lifts into your tray. Slide while holding, or touch a pinned piece, and the glass clinks, costing a Steady Hand heart. Plain glass, gold-flecked glass and rare meenakari score 10, 25 and 50 points. It has an Afternoon mode with rounds and hearts, a two-player pass-and-play mode, and a Zen mode. It is free, works offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

How to play

  • Lift: press and hold a piece that nothing rests on. A brass ring fills around your finger; when it closes the piece glides into the brass tray and scores. A piece touching nothing lifts almost at once; a piece resting against neighbours needs a longer hold.
  • Stay still: sliding your finger while holding swells the red disc inside the ring. Fill it and the pile wobbles: that is a clink. Lift your finger before the ring closes and nothing happens.
  • Read the pile: pieces land one after another; the later a piece lands, the higher it sits. A deep, soft shadow means a piece is on top; a piece cut by another piece's shadow is underneath.
  • The clink: touch a pinned piece and everything on top of it jostles. In an Afternoon run that costs one of three hearts; in two-player it passes the turn; in Zen nothing happens.
  • Hint: glints the edges of every free piece for a moment. Three per round, unlimited in Zen.
  • Re-scatter: drops the pile again. Allowed until your first pick of a round, any time in Zen.
  • Points: plain glass 10, gold-flecked 25, meenakari 50. Five clean lifts in a row add +5 to each pick, ten add +10. A round with no clink earns a 100-point clean sweep.
  • Rounds: clearing the floor mends one heart and starts a larger, tighter heap that asks for a longer, stiller hold. Three clinks end the afternoon.

Common questions

I touched a piece and it clinked at once, but it looked free. The game chooses the piece whose glass is under your finger, top-most first. Look at the shadows: a piece partly covered by another piece's shadow is underneath it. Hint shows exactly which pieces are free.

The ring went red and the pile wobbled. Your finger slid while holding. Rest your hand, press straight down and wait for the ring to close. Pieces touching neighbours allow less movement, and later Afternoon rounds allow less still; Zen is the most forgiving.

Nothing happens when I press. The pile must finish settling first (a few seconds after the scatter). Presses on the tray and on bare floor do nothing, and lifting your finger before the ring closes cancels the lift.

Is every board solvable? Yes. The piece that landed last is always free, so lifting from the top down always clears the floor.

Where did my run go? Afternoon runs are saved between rounds. "Save & leave" in the pause menu keeps the round and your hearts; the unfinished round's points are dropped and the round is scattered again when you continue. "Give up run" ends it.

Can I turn off the shaking, sparkles or sounds? Yes. Settings has switches for sound effects, ambience, haptics and Reduce motion, and lets you choose the terracotta courtyard or the woven chatai mat.

Does it work on iPad? Yes. The courtyard grows with the screen in portrait.

Saving and progress

Your bangle jar (pieces lifted per colour, collector title), best score, best round, best streak, counters, the saved Afternoon run, whether the first-time card was shown, and your settings are saved on your device after every round and when the app goes to the background. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress empties the jar on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Kaanch works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
