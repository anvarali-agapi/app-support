Kaanch

Privacy policy · effective 12 November 2026

Summary

Kaanch does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small save on your device so your progress survives between sessions. It contains only game data:

  • best score, best round and best streak in Afternoon mode
  • counters: runs played, two-player games, Zen boards, pieces lifted in total and per colour, meenakari pieces found, clinks
  • the saved Afternoon run, if any: round, score, hearts and pieces lifted
  • whether the first-time "how to lift" card has been shown
  • your settings: sound effects, ambience, haptics, reduce motion and floor style

This data never leaves your device. It is not linked to you, and deleting the app deletes it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
  • It writes to the clipboard only when you tap a link in Settings › About to copy it; it never reads the clipboard.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Kaanch is a gentle puzzle about picking up glass bangle pieces, with no violence, chat or links to outside content beyond this policy. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, and the app's listing will link to it.
