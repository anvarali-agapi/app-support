Shellbound Siege

Tower defense with a sweet tooth. Guard the Peanut Jar from bandit squirrels, crows and raccoons.

How do I play?

Tap a stone plot to build a tower, then tap Start Wave. Towers fire on their own, and every bandit they stop earns you peanuts. Spend peanuts on more towers or tap a tower to upgrade it. Each wave is a little tougher than the last. Keep the jar from being emptied to win.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Stars and unlocked levels are saved on your device automatically. Deleting the app removes them. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between devices.

Can I replay the tutorial?

Open Settings and tap Replay tutorial. It plays the next time you start Backyard Burrow.

How do I turn off sound or music?

Use the switches in Settings on the title screen.
