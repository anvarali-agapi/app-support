Privacy Policy

Shellbound Siege · Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

Shellbound Siege does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things locally so it works the way you left it: your best stars per level, which levels are unlocked, whether sound and music are on, and whether you have finished the tutorial. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

The game asks for no special permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, the game is suitable for players of all ages, and no data from children is gathered.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
