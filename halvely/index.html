Slice & pour craft puzzle · support

What it is

Halvely is a cozy, satisfying craft puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Android. At a warm hobby desk you cut soap, kinetic sand, candle wax, clay and resin into exact halves, requested shares and equal thirds with one straight stroke, and you pour liquids into glass vessels to meet the etched line. Five craft books hold sixty hand-made pieces, plus a daily set of five and an endless Free Craft mode. It is free, works offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

How to play

  • Slicing: drag a straight line across the piece and lift your finger. The dotted guide shows where the blade will go. Cut it exactly in half, cut the requested share (for example 25% from 75%), or make two cuts for three equal pieces. For three pieces the second cut must pass through one piece only.
  • Pins: clay slabs hide brass pins; a cut that touches a pin spoils the piece. Drifting pieces: kinetic sand slides along the tray, so time the cut.
  • Pouring: touch and hold the jug to tip it, let go to stop. Slide the tray underneath to bring a vessel under the stream. Fill each vessel to its etched line; the faint green band is the Flawless tolerance.
  • Thick liquids: wax and resin keep landing for a moment after you let go, so stop early. Over the rim is an overflow and the piece is spoiled.
  • Mixing: in the Resin Studio two jugs hold two tints. Match each vessel's colour chip by pouring from both, then meet the line.
  • Grades: pieces are measured in percentage points off the target. Slicing: within 1.5% is Flawless (three stars), 4% Neat, 9% Rough. Pouring: 2%, 5%, 10%. A Flawless piece joins the Shelf.

Common questions

The next book is locked. Finish nine of the twelve pieces in the current book (any grade) to open the next one.

My cut did nothing. The line must cross the piece; a stroke that misses only makes it wobble. Very short strokes are ignored, so draw a clear line.

The jug ran dry before the last vessel. The jug holds only a little more than the lines need. A heavy pour early leaves a short pour later; use Retry.

The second cut made four pieces. After the first cut the halves slide apart. Aim the second cut through one of them only.

I missed a day. Is my streak gone? Yes, the Daily Craft streak counts consecutive days. Your days-made total and best day are kept.

Can I turn off the shake and flashes? Yes. Settings has a Calm mode, and separate switches for sound effects, room ambience and haptics.

Does it work on iPad? Yes, the layout adapts to iPhone and iPad screens in portrait.

Saving and progress

Your desk (grades and best accuracy for every piece, the shelf, daily streak and history, Free Craft counters and settings) is saved on your device after every finished piece and when the app goes to the background. A piece in progress is not saved; leaving it returns you to the book. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes it on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Halvely works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
