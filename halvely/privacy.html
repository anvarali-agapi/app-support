Halvely

Privacy policy · effective 9 November 2026

Summary

Halvely does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small save on your device so your desk survives between sessions. It contains only game data:

  • for each piece: the best grade, the best accuracy and how many times it was played
  • Daily Craft: the current streak, the date of the last completed day, the best day, up to sixty recent day results, and the accuracy of today's unfinished pieces
  • Free Craft: pieces made and best run of neat pieces
  • counters: cuts made, pours made, flawless pieces and minutes played
  • whether the three first-time lessons (slicing, pouring, mixing) have been shown
  • your settings: sound effects, room ambience, haptics and Calm mode

This data never leaves your device. It is not linked to you, and deleting the app deletes it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • It does not connect to the internet. The daily pieces are computed on your device from the calendar date.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
  • It writes to the clipboard only when you tap a link in Settings › About to copy it; it never reads the clipboard.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Halvely is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, and the app's listing will link to it.
