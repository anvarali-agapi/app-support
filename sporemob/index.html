What it is

Sporemob is a one-thumb crowd-runner turf war for iPhone, iPad and Android, set on a miniature forest floor. You are a glowing blue mushroom colony; a rotting stump at the end of each lane pours out magenta mold. Hold to spray cap minions up the lane and drag to steer them through dew fronds that split the swarm, sunbeams that fuse caps into towering Goliaths, bark barricades, moss tufts and mold slicks. Spores that reach the stump decompose it; mold that reaches the bottom eats your roots. Thirty lanes in five chapters, an endless Overrun mode and a Nursery of upgrades. It is free, works offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

How to play

  • Spray and steer: touch and hold anywhere on the lane. The Mother Cap at the bottom sprays caps while you hold and slides left and right under your finger. Let go and it rests.
  • Burst: the ring under the Mother Cap fills while you are not spraying (slowly while you are). When it glows, your next touch releases a fan of caps at once.
  • Dew fronds (x2, x3): every cap that crosses splits. Chain two fronds and ten caps become forty. Some fronds drift sideways; follow them. Goliaths are too big to split.
  • Moss tufts (+N): the first cap to touch one shakes loose extra caps; the tuft regrows for a few seconds.
  • Sunbeams: absorb the caps that enter them. When the ring fills (8 caps, fewer with the Goliath Growth upgrade) one Spore Goliath rises: slow, huge, crushes mites and bites deep into the stump.
  • Bark barricades: caps die against bark and chip a point each. A Goliath smashes through if its power finishes the wall; otherwise it is spent.
  • Mold slicks (1/2): drown every second cap that crosses and burn a third of a Goliath. Steer around them.
  • The mold: mites trickle down, wisps rush, beetle champions soak up a stream. Spores and mold trade blows one for one. Anything that reaches the bottom bites your roots.
  • Stumps: every spore that reaches a Rotting Stump Core takes its power off the number above it. At zero it is yours. A badly hurt stump surges with mold once. Some lanes hold several stumps: rot them all.
  • Stars and dust: finish with 70% of your roots for three stars, 35% for two. Lanes pay Spore Dust (less on replays); spend it in the Nursery.

Common questions

My spores vanish in the sunbeam. That is the sunbeam absorbing them. Keep the stream on it until the ring is full and a Goliath rises. Caps are only wasted if you leave before it fills; the count stays until you come back.

The Goliath died on the bark. The number on the plank is the wall's health. If the Goliath's power is lower, weaken the wall with caps first (each chips a point) or grow stronger Goliaths in the Nursery.

I lose roots even though I am winning. Mold that slips past the stream still reaches you. Keep the Mother Cap under the biggest group of mites and save Bursts for beetle champions; their health bar shows how much is left.

A lane feels impossible. The campaign expects a few Nursery upgrades by chapter two. Replay earlier lanes for dust (they pay about half), buy Cap Vigor or Goliath Growth, and come back.

Where is Overrun? It unlocks after lane 1-6, the first boss stump. Roots carry over between stumps and heal 15% each time.

Can I turn off the shaking or the sounds? Yes. Settings has switches for sound effects, ambience, haptics and Reduce motion (no screen shake, calmer light, fewer pollen motes).

Does it work on iPad? Yes. The lane fills the screen in portrait and the text scales up.

Saving and progress

Your stars per lane, Spore Dust, Nursery levels, Overrun bests, lifetime counters, whether the field guide was shown, and your settings are saved on your device after every lane and when the app goes to the background. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes it on purpose (settings stay).

Offline and accounts

Sporemob works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
