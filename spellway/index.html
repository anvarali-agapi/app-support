Spellway

Drive the Word. Steer your car into letter blocks to spell the word at the top of the screen, dodge the wrong ones, boost through barriers and race a boss at the end of every world.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to steer between the three lanes. The letter you need next glows; hit it to add it to the word. Red-tinted letters are wrong and cost fuel, and so do barriers. Catching correct letters refills fuel and charges the boost meter: tap to boost for double points and to smash through barriers. Finish every word to reach the finish line. One mistake or fewer earns three stars, four or fewer earns two.

What are the world twists?

Each world changes the road: sliding traffic walls in Neon Harbor, sand patches in Dune Drift, slippery ice in Frostline, ramps and sky coins in Canopy Run, falling lava rocks in Ember Peak and drifting zero-G letters in Orbit Lane. A short tip appears the first time each twist shows up. Level 15 of every world is a timed boss chase with long words.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Stars, coins, cars, achievements and your daily streak are saved automatically on your device. Progress is not synced between devices, and deleting the app removes it. You can reset it any time in Settings.

Daily rewards

Claim one reward per calendar day. The calendar grows for seven days in a row; missing a day restarts it from day 1. If the device clock is moved backwards, claiming pauses until the real time catches up.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Spellway works fully offline, with no account, no ads, no purchases and no tracking. All art, sound effects and music are generated by the game itself.

Reporting a problem

Email kristidenna@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, the world and level number, and describe what happened. We usually reply within a few days.
