Gearlorn

Privacy policy · effective 7 November 2026

Summary

Gearlorn does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small save on your device so your expedition survives between sessions. It contains only game data:

  • the room you are in and where you entered it, your current scale and pace
  • relics found, cogs collected, keys held, iron doors opened, levers thrown, walls smashed, gaslamps lit and rooms charted
  • counters: steps taken, times undone, play time, whether the Escapement has been restarted
  • your settings: music, sound effects, haptics and swipe-to-walk

This data never leaves your device. It is not linked to you, and deleting the app deletes it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
  • It does not read your clipboard or any file outside its own save.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Gearlorn is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, and the app's listing will link to it.
