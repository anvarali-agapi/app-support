What it is

Gearlorn is a clockwork puzzle metroidvania for iPhone, iPad and Android. You are Wick, the last wound lamplighter of a gothic foundry whose Great Escapement has stopped. Every step you take is one beat of the Foundry, and every machine keeps that beat. Shrink to a mite or grow to a colossus, set your pace, and find the way through 22 connected rooms. It is free, offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

Contact support

Found a bug, stuck in a room, or have an idea? Open an issue and we will answer there. Please include your device model, your iOS or Android version, and the name of the room (shown at the top of the screen).

How to play

  • Swipe on the board, or tap the arrows, to walk one tile. The centre button waits one beat.
  • Machines move only when you do. The four lamps at the top show the current beat. Brass pistons shut on beat 4, copper ones on beat 2; steam vents are live on beat 4; brass gear bridges hold on beats 1 and 2, iron ones on 3 and 4.
  • Walk into a wooden crate to push it. A crate, a sentry or Wick on a pressure plate keeps its gate open. A crate on a piston jams it open.
  • Scale: once you have the Scale Valve, tap the small figure to shrink to a mite (fits through grates, slips under shut pistons, cannot push). With the Boiler Heart, tap the large figure to grow to a colossus (pushes iron crates, breaks cracked brick, immune to steam, crushes sentries, but breaks planks, ignores belts and cannot pass arches).
  • Pace: once you have the Tempo Dial, pick Crawl (a beat every second step), Steady (one beat) or Rush (two beats; only the beat you land on can hurt you).
  • Sentries march one tile per beat along a fixed round. Touching one sends Wick back to the door, unless Wick is a colossus.
  • Light gaslamps to set your return point and to travel between them from the chart (map icon). Levers lock open shortcuts for good.

Common questions

I keep getting scalded or crushed. Count the beats. Step onto a vent on beats 1 to 3, never 4. Never stand under a brass piston on beat 4 or a copper one on beat 2. The Wait button lets a beat pass without moving.

A crate fell into the dark and I cannot finish the room. Use Reset room (below the scale control, or in the pause menu). Crates return; relics, cogs and lit lamps are kept.

The belt keeps pushing me back. Walk against a belt at Crawl pace, or as a colossus, which belts cannot move.

Where is the Spire? Behind the cracked wall at the top of the Escapement Works. It needs a colossus.

How many cogs are there? Twelve. The Archive lists which you have found and which room still hides one.

Can I turn off the sound or swiping? Yes. Music, sound effects, haptics and swipe-to-walk each have a switch in Settings.

Does it work on iPad? Yes, the layout adapts to iPhone and iPad screens in portrait.

Saving and progress

Your expedition (relics, cogs, keys, opened doors, thrown levers, smashed walls, lit lamps, charted rooms and the room you are in) is saved on your device after every room, pickup and lamp, and when the app goes to the background. Crate positions inside a room are not saved: you return to the door you came in by. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes it on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Gearlorn works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
