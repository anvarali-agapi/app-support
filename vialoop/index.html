What it is

Vialoop is a cozy clockwork apothecary puzzle for iPhone, iPad and Android. Brass-and-glass vials stand under a wooden conveyor loop. Tap a vial and its top beads of one colour leap onto the belt, ride the loop and drop into the first open vial whose top bead matches their colour. Gather every colour into a single vial before the loom overloads. Some beads carry an Ember charge that melts the sealed covers of neighbouring vials, and some carry Frost that ices a vial over for a few seconds. It has a 60-level journey through five chambers and an endless Night Shift mode. It is free, works offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

How to play

  • Launch: tap a vial. Its top run of one colour flies up into the loop above it and rides the belt leftwards along the bottom rail, up the left side, across the top and back down the right.
  • Find a home: a bead drops into the first open vial it passes whose top bead shares its colour. Empty vials catch any colour. Full, sealed and frozen vials are passed by, and the bead keeps circling until something opens.
  • Gather every colour: the level ends the moment each colour rests in one vial and the belt is empty. Which vial holds which colour is up to you.
  • Sealed glass and Ember: a frosted plate bolted over a vial seals it: nothing in, nothing out. When a bead marked with a flame lands in a vial, the covers on both neighbouring vials melt away.
  • Frost: when a bead marked with a snowflake lands, its vial ices over for five seconds: no launches out and no beads in. Anything heading there keeps circling.
  • Loom load: the brass dial counts beads riding the belt and waiting in the chutes. Launch more than it can carry and the loom jams; the level is lost and you can try again. A bead that circles twice without a home glows red.
  • Par and stars: par is the apothecary's own count of launches. Match it for three stars; stay within half again for two; finish for one.
  • Hints: three per level. They work whenever the belt is clear and point at a vial worth tapping.
  • Night Shift: endless rounds that add colours, taller vials, covers, frost and a faster belt. Points for every bead gathered and every launch under par. One jam ends the shift.

Common questions

My bead went into the wrong vial. Beads always take the first open vial they pass, counting leftwards from the vial they left, then around the loop. If a vial to the left has the same colour on top and room to spare, the bead lands there. Turn on "Show landing vial" in Settings (it is on by default) and the vial a run is heading for glows for a moment after you tap.

Nothing happens when I tap a vial. Sealed vials, frozen vials and empty vials cannot be launched; the vial shakes and a label says why. Melt a seal with an Ember landing next door; frost thaws by itself after five seconds.

The loom jammed the moment I tapped. The launch would have put more beads on the belt than the dial allows. Let circling beads land first, or open a vial for them, before launching another run.

It says nothing can move. Every open vial is empty and the beads on the belt have no matching vial to fall into. Restart the level; every level in the journey has been checked to be solvable.

Is every level solvable? Yes. Every journey level and every Night Shift layout was solved by the game's own solver before being included.

Can I turn off the sparks, shaking or sounds? Yes. Settings has switches for sound effects, ambience, haptics and Reduce motion.

Does it work on iPad? Yes. The apparatus grows with the screen in portrait, with larger type.

Saving and progress

Your stars and best launch count per level, Night Shift bests, the ledger counters (levels cleared, beads gathered, covers melted, shifts worked), which one-time tips you have seen, and your settings are saved on your device after every level and when the app goes to the background. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes the ledger on purpose; settings are kept.

Offline and accounts

Vialoop works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
