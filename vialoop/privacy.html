Vialoop

Privacy policy · effective 15 November 2026

Summary

Vialoop does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases and makes no network connections. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

The app saves game progress in its private storage on your device, using the operating system's standard app preferences:

  • stars and best launch count for each journey level, and your Night Shift best rounds and best score;
  • ledger counters: levels cleared, beads gathered, covers melted and Night Shifts worked;
  • which one-time tips you have already seen;
  • your settings: sound effects, ambience, haptics, Reduce motion and Show landing vial.

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app, and you can wipe the progress yourself with Settings › Reset all progress.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, identifiers or any other personal information.
  • It does not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not connect to the internet and does not transmit anything.
  • It does not request any device permissions beyond haptic feedback, which needs none.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Vialoop is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the support issue tracker.
