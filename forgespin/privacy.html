Forgespin

Privacy policy · effective 11 November 2026

Summary

Forgespin does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small save on your device so your progress survives between sessions. It contains only game data:

  • for each campaign duel: the best star rating
  • your slag balance, the parts you own, the temper level of each slot and the parts you have equipped
  • Crucible: best wave and number of runs
  • counters: duels played and won, vents, meltdowns, times shattered and seconds played
  • whether the welcome, forge and first-duel hints have been shown
  • your settings: sound effects, ambience, haptics, Steady mode and vent button side

This data never leaves your device. It is not linked to you, and deleting the app deletes it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it on request.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use any device permission (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos).
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
  • It writes to the clipboard only when you tap a link in Settings › About to copy it; it never reads the clipboard.

Because nothing leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children

Forgespin contains stylised fantasy combat between spinning tops and no real-world violence. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
