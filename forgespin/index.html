What it is

Forgespin is a dark-fantasy spinning-top duel for iPhone, iPad and Android. You steer a forged top across a basalt arena ringed with lava, grind against rivals to build heat, go molten for crushing knockback at the cost of a brittle core, and vent before the gauge fills. Five foundries hold thirty named rivals and five bosses; the Crucible is an endless wave mode; the Forge lets you build your top from blades, cores and weights. It is free, works offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

How to play

  • Steer: drag anywhere on the arena. A stick appears under your finger; push it where you want your top to roll. Steering costs a little spin.
  • Heat: clashing and grinding against a rival heats both of you. Hot blades hit harder. Away from rivals, heat cools slowly; quench grates cool it fast; lava vents and magma rings heat it.
  • Molten: at 75 heat your blades go molten: your knockback soars, but your core turns brittle and every hit you take deals far more damage.
  • Vent: tap the round VENT button to drop your heat to zero in one shockwave. The hotter you were, the further it reaches and the harder it throws rivals. It needs 20 heat and has a short cooldown.
  • Meltdown: if the gauge reaches 100 your core melts down: you lose a chunk of integrity and stall for a moment.
  • Three ways to lose: shatter (integrity at zero), topple (spin runs out), or the lava moat (thrown off the floor). The same three end a rival.
  • Stars: one for the win, one for keeping half your integrity, one for never melting down.

Common questions

The next foundry is locked. Beat the sixth duel (the boss) of the previous foundry to open it. Duels inside a foundry unlock one after another.

I keep shattering. You are taking hits while hot. Vent earlier, roll over a quench grate, or forge more integrity (Pit Disc, Anvil Ring, Cold Iron Core). Steady mode in Settings also removes the slow motion if that distracts you.

I keep toppling. Spin runs out faster when you steer constantly and grind for long. Clash in short bursts, or forge the Hollow Steel core or Chainlink Ring.

The vent button does nothing. It needs at least 20 heat and a free cooldown. Its ring shows your heat; the dark sweep shows the cooldown.

Where do relics come from? Five parts cannot be bought: each foundry boss drops one on your first win. The Forge names the boss for each.

Can I turn off slow motion, shake and flashes? Yes. Settings has a Steady mode, and separate switches for sound effects, ambience and haptics. The vent button can be moved to the left.

Does it work on iPad? Yes, the layout adapts to iPhone and iPad screens in portrait.

Saving and progress

Your foundry (stars for every duel, slag, owned parts, temper levels, the equipped build, Crucible best and records, and settings) is saved on your device after every duel and purchase, and when the app goes to the background. A duel in progress is not saved; leaving it counts as nothing. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes it on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Forgespin works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
