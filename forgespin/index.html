What it is

Forgespin is a dark-fantasy spinning-top battle royale for iPhone, iPad and Android. You wind up and launch a forged top once, with timing and aim, into a walled basalt arena. After that nobody steers: tops curve, wander, bounce off the iron wall and clash. Every clash costs spin, grinding builds heat, hot blades hit harder and brittle molten cores shatter. The last top still spinning wins. Five foundries hold thirty duels against crowds of up to seven rivals and five bosses; the Crucible is an endless wave mode; the Forge lets you build your top from blades, cores and weights. It is free, works offline, and has no ads, purchases or accounts.

How to play

  • Launch: hold anywhere to wind up. The meter runs up and down; release at the peak (90% or more) for a perfect launch. Drag while holding to aim; with no drag you launch toward the centre.
  • Hands off: after the launch there is no control. Tops curve and wander, the bowl pulls them together, and the iron wall throws them back. Every clash slows both tops; the lighter, weaker one loses more spin.
  • Heat: clashing and grinding heats both tops. Hot blades hit harder. Away from rivals heat cools slowly; quench grates cool it fast; lava vents and magma rings heat it.
  • Molten: at 75 heat blades go molten: knockback soars, but the core turns brittle and every hit deals far more damage.
  • Meltdown: if the gauge reaches 100 the core melts down: a chunk of integrity is lost, the top stalls and loses spin. A Rune Edge blade vents itself at 90 instead.
  • Two ways out: shatter (integrity at zero) or topple (spin runs out). The last top still spinning wins; if yours stops while any rival still turns, the duel is lost.
  • Stars: one for the win, one for keeping half your integrity, one for a perfect launch.

Common questions

The next foundry is locked. Beat the sixth duel (the boss) of the previous foundry to open it. Duels inside a foundry unlock one after another.

I can't steer after the launch. That is the game: one launch, then physics. Your choices are the timing and aim of the launch and the top you forged.

I keep shattering. You are taking hits while hot. Aim your launch through a quench grate or away from the pack, and forge more integrity or a cooler core (Pit Disc, Anvil Ring, Cold Iron Core). Steady mode in Settings removes the slow motion if that distracts you.

I keep toppling. Spin runs out faster in a pile of rivals and for light tops. Wind up to the peak, aim away from the crowd, and forge stamina and mass (Hollow Steel, Chainlink Ring, Cursed Weight).

Where do relics come from? Five parts cannot be bought: each foundry boss drops one on your first win. The Forge names the boss for each.

Can I turn off slow motion, shake and flashes? Yes. Settings has a Steady mode, and separate switches for sound effects, ambience and haptics.

Does it work on iPad? Yes, the layout adapts to iPhone and iPad screens in portrait.

Saving and progress

Your foundry (stars for every duel, slag, owned parts, temper levels, the equipped build, Crucible best and records, and settings) is saved on your device after every duel and purchase, and when the app goes to the background. A duel in progress is not saved; leaving it counts as nothing. There is no cloud sync, so deleting the app deletes the save. Settings › Reset all progress wipes it on purpose.

Offline and accounts

Forgespin works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no advertising and nothing to buy.
