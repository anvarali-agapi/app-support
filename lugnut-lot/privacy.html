Privacy Policy

Lugnut Lot · Effective October 29, 2026

Summary

Lugnut Lot needs no account and asks for no personal details. It shows no ads and sells nothing. The only data that may leave your device is anonymous usage statistics, described below.

What the app stores on your device

To remember your game, the app saves your progress (lots cleared, stars, coins, boosters and the daily gift) and your settings (music, sound effects and haptics) locally on your device. This data is not linked to you and is deleted when you delete the app or reset progress in Settings.

Analytics

The app may send anonymous usage events, such as a lot being started, cleared or failed and a booster being used, to Google Firebase Analytics so we can see where players get stuck and tune the game. These events are not linked to your name, email or any account, and we do not use them for advertising or tracking across apps. Google's privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No in-app purchases and no payment details
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no selling of data
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
