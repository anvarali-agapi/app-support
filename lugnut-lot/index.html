Lugnut Lot

Unscrew the plates, free the cars, clear the lot. A parking-lot order puzzle for iPhone and iPad.

Need help or found a bug?

Open a support request and describe what happened. Please include your device model, iOS version and the lot number if your question is about a specific puzzle. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap a car to drive it out. If something is in the way it bumps, and a bump costs a strike. Three strikes and the lot is lost; no bumps earns three stars.

Red knobs hold plates. Unscrew every knob on a plate and it pops away. Knobs under cars come free when that car leaves, and clamped cars need their clamp unscrewed first. Chain clean exits for combos.

From chapter two you have a wrench budget: only so many screws per lot, so pick the ones that matter. The first lots walk you through each idea.

Coins and boosters

Coins come from clearing lots (more stars, more coins) and from the daily gift. Spend them on boosters from the tray under the board: a hint that points at the right tap, a shield that forgives one bump, or an extra wrench. There is nothing to buy with real money and no ads.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your lots, stars, coins and boosters are saved automatically on your device. Progress is not synced between devices, and deleting the app removes it. You can reset it any time in Settings.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Lugnut Lot needs no account or sign-in and plays fully offline. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.
