LUMOCHET NEON RIFT

Bend light. Break the rift. A neon ricochet arcade game.

How do I play?

Drag upward anywhere in the arena to trace a bank-shot guide, then release to launch your whole orb array. Orbs ricochet off the walls and chip away at hex-cells; the number on a cell is how many hits it has left. Where the first orb returns is where you launch from next. Tap the ? on the home screen for the full guide.

What are the different cells?

Cyan prism cells are plain. Violet armor cells carry a shield that must be knocked off first. Amber burst cells explode and damage their neighbours. Green sentinels drift sideways across your shot path. Every fifth wave brings a pink rift core: breach it and your array grows by two orbs.

What is Prism Surge?

Hitting cells and catching pink rift diamonds fills the charge bar. At 100%, tap Surge before launching and every orb in that volley deals double damage. Every third diamond caught in one volley also adds an orb, as do the cyan plus pickups.

What are rift shards and the Prism Core?

Shards are banked at the end of every run. Spend them on the home screen to upgrade your Prism Core, which raises the number of orbs you start each run with, up to level six.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes your best score, shards and upgrades.

Long volleys feel slow

Volleys speed up on their own: twice as fast after 10 seconds and four times as fast after 18 seconds, so a volley never drags on.
