Privacy Policy

Lumochet: Neon Rift · Effective 7 November 2026

The short version

Lumochet: Neon Rift does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

  • Your best score and best wave.
  • Your rift shard balance and Prism Core upgrade level.
  • Your settings: whether sound is on, and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This information is saved only in the app's own storage on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network and permissions

The game works fully offline and makes no network connections. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

Children

Because no personal data is collected, the game is suitable for players of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
